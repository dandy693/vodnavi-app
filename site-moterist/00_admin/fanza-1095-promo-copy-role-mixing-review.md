# FANZA 1095 Promo Copy Role Mixing Review

## 1. Purpose

This document reviews whether strong promotional wording on page `1095` creates role mixing with `954 Evergreen Sale Hub`.

Its goals are:

- evaluate whether current promo wording is acceptable inside a `Beginner Guide`
- identify where promo intensity risks turning `1095` into a sale-first route
- define what should remain, be weakened, shift toward `954`, or become a later removal candidate

This review is local-only and does not authorize production edits.

## 2. Target Page Information

- `page_id`: `1095`
- `page_type`: `Beginner Guide`
- `page_role`: `初心者導入・不安低減・登録導線`

Role boundary reminder:

- beginner onboarding must remain dominant
- anxiety reduction must come before stronger conversion pressure
- official FANZA guidance must remain primary
- `954` sale-confirmation behavior must remain secondary on this page

## 3. Current Decision

- current status: `HOLD`

Other strong promotional wording identified in the same public pass:

These phrases were observed in promotional / affiliate areas of public output.

## 5. Review Locations

Observed from the read-only public HTML pass:

- promotional affiliate block areas in the public page output
- CTA / support-link neighborhood where beginner guidance and sales-oriented prompts can be read in the same session

Known surrounding route context:

- beginner article body
- support links to `1106 / 994 / 954`
- official CTA for latest FANZA information

## 6. Acceptability Inside A Beginner Guide

Current assessment:

- partially acceptable only if clearly subordinate
- not acceptable if the wording becomes the main motivational hook

Reason:

- `1095` is not the commercial sale hub
- strong discount-led wording risks moving the page from orientation into urgency-first behavior
- `954` is the designated page type for current sale checking and campaign emphasis

## 7. Role-Mixing Risk With `954 Evergreen Sale Hub`

Risk level:

- `HOLD` risk, material but not yet conclusive `NO-GO`

Why:

- `954` is designed to own sale-check intent
- `1095` is designed to introduce the service safely and reduce beginner confusion
- `90%OFFクーポン` language can pull user attention into active-offer intent before beginner orientation is complete

Role-mixing indicators observed:

- explicit discount emphasis
- campaign / coupon framing
- commercially strong affiliate tone

## 8. Conditions Under Which The Promo Copy May Remain On `1095`

It may remain only if all of the following are true:

- the wording is visually weaker than the official beginner-oriented CTA
- the wording is clearly separated from the article’s core explanatory flow
- the wording does not dominate the top reading path
- the page still reads first as `FANZAとは？初心者向け…`
- the sale / coupon wording is treated as a secondary reference, not the primary promise
- the wording is current and not stale

## 9. Conditions Under Which It Should Be Weakened On `1095`

It should be weakened if any of the following is true:

- the wording functions only as supplementary information that lowers beginner anxiety
- the wording visually competes with the official CTA
- the wording pulls attention before the user understands what FANZA is
- the wording is stronger than the support route to `954`
- the wording makes the article feel more promotional than explanatory

Recommended weakening directions:

- reduce discount-first emphasis
- remove urgency-first phrasing
- reframe as a secondary “latest offer check” note

## 10. Conditions Under Which It Should Shift Toward `954`

It should be treated as `954`-owned intent if:

- the sale message is acting as the star of the page rather than a support note
- the user motivation implied by the wording is “what deal is active now?”
- the wording functions as a live sale / coupon check
- the wording is better interpreted as campaign-sensitive than evergreen beginner guidance
- freshness matters more than explanation

Implication:

- `1095` should point toward `954` rather than trying to carry strong sale-copy itself

## 11. Conditions Under Which Removal Or Replacement Should Be Considered

Consider removal or replacement if:

- the wording is stale or cannot be validated
- the wording causes clear role drift into sale-hub behavior
- the wording visually overpowers beginner onboarding
- the wording weakens the page’s anxiety-reduction purpose
- the wording is the strongest commercial message on the page

## 12. Conditions That Keep `HOLD`

Keep `HOLD` if:

- wording freshness is not yet validated
- rendered hierarchy is not yet checked
- the actual visual dominance of the promo block is unknown
- the page-level effect on beginner orientation cannot yet be concluded safely
- the wording may already be creating an “今すぐセール” feeling but its actual weight is not yet fully judged

## 13. Conditions That Switch To `NO-GO`

Switch to `NO-GO` if any of the following becomes true:

- the promo wording is stale
- the promo wording clearly makes `1095` function like `954`
- the promo wording overtakes the official beginner-oriented CTA
- the promo wording causes the page to become sale-first before orientation
- the page gives a strong “今すぐセール” impression before beginner onboarding is complete
- the promo wording introduces unsupported urgency or certainty claims

## 14. Recommended Handling

Current recommendation:

- recommended action: `954への内部導線に寄せる`

Reason:

- `90%OFFクーポン` and similarly strong offer-led wording better match the sale-check intent owned by `954`
- `1095` should preserve a beginner-first orientation and use sale language only as a secondary support route

Secondary recommendation:

- if the wording functions mainly as supplementary information that lowers beginner anxiety, treat it as a weakening candidate rather than a stable keeper

Not recommended at current knowledge level:

- `そのまま残す`

Conditional option:

- `削除候補にする`
  - if freshness cannot be validated
  - if rendered hierarchy shows promo dominance
