# FANZA 1095 FAQ Necessity / Redundancy Review

This document reviews whether the FAQ layer on `1095` is still appropriate for the page's `Beginner Guide` role, or whether it is becoming redundant, overlapping, or role-mixing relative to adjacent priority pages.

## 2. Target Page Information

- ID: `1095`
- page type: `Beginner Guide`
- page role: `初心者導入・不安低減・登録導線`

## 3. Current Decision

- `HOLD`

Reason:

- FAQ intent is directionally understandable
- actual necessity and rendered hierarchy are not yet fully proven
- overlap with `994 / 1106 / 954` must still be judged conservatively

## 4. FAQ Expected Role

Within `1095`, the FAQ should:

- answer lightweight beginner hesitation
- support the final orientation step after the main guide flow
- reduce small remaining uncertainty without becoming the main reassurance asset

The FAQ should not:

- become the page's main trust block
- behave like a benefits explainer
- behave like a current-sale confirmation block
- compete with the primary CTA

## 5. Whether The FAQ Contributes To Beginner Onboarding

Provisional judgment:

- `provisionally yes`

Reason:

- beginner-oriented FAQ can help users who still have small procedural hesitation after reading the main body
- draft FAQ intent is consistent with `利用前に何を確認しておくべきか` type questions

Constraint:

- it contributes only when the answers remain short, practical, and subordinate to the main onboarding flow

## 6. Whether The FAQ Contributes To Anxiety Reduction

Provisional judgment:

- `provisionally yes`

Reason:

- a short FAQ can reduce minor uncertainty
- this is valid only if the FAQ does not become the primary reassurance mechanism

Boundary:

- if the FAQ carries deeper trust recovery, it starts drifting toward `994`

## 7. Overlap Risk With 994 Safety / Anxiety Resolution

Current risk:

- `medium`

Why:

- both pages can touch hesitation and uncertainty
- `1095` should handle light orientation anxiety
- `994` should handle deeper safety and anxiety resolution

Risk increases if FAQ answers:

- become safety-heavy
- become trust-restoration heavy
- repeat deep reassurance that belongs on `994`

## 8. Overlap Risk With 1106 Registration / Benefits Guide

Current risk:

- `low to medium`

Why:

- `1106` is benefits-first
- `1095` should not let FAQ drift into a reasons-to-register or perks-led explanation area

Risk increases if FAQ answers:

- emphasize benefits or registration perks too strongly
- explain value propositions instead of beginner next steps
- behave like a merits-focused secondary section

## 9. Role-Mixing Risk With 954 Evergreen Sale Hub

Current risk:

- `medium to high`

Why:

- `954` owns sale-check intent
- `1095` should not let FAQ become a soft sale or coupon block

Risk increases if FAQ answers:

- push current deals
- highlight coupon urgency
- encourage current sale confirmation before beginner orientation is complete

## 10. Whether Sale / Benefit Promotion Is Too Strong Inside The FAQ

Current provisional conclusion:

- `must stay tightly limited`

Rules:

- FAQ may mention next-step sale confirmation only as a secondary route
- FAQ must not make discount or perks the main action driver
- FAQ must not behave like a promotion block

If FAQ answers use strong offer-led framing, treat that as `HOLD` or possible `NO-GO`.

## 11. Whether The FAQ Disrupts The CTA Flow

Current provisional conclusion:

- `not proven safe yet`

Source-level expectation:

- FAQ should appear only after the primary route is already understandable
- FAQ should remain supportive, not competitive

Open issue:

- rendered hierarchy is still unproven
- therefore actual CTA interference remains a `HOLD` issue

## 12. Conditions To Keep The FAQ On 1095

Keep the FAQ on `1095` only if:

- it supports beginner orientation
- it answers light hesitation rather than deep trust concerns
- it stays weaker than the main CTA
- it does not behave like a sale or benefits block
- it does not duplicate `994`
- it does not duplicate `1106`
- it does not behave like `954`

## 13. Conditions To Weaken The FAQ

Weaken the FAQ if:

- it is useful but longer than needed
- it repeats content already clear in the body
- it adds too much density after the CTA route is already clear
- it begins to carry too much reassurance or promo weight

## 14. Conditions To Shift Toward 994

Shift the topic toward `994` if:

- the FAQ question is mainly about safety or anxiety resolution
- the answer requires deeper reassurance than `1095` should carry
- the answer makes `1095` too similar to `994`

## 15. Conditions To Mark As Removal Or Replacement Candidate

Mark the FAQ as a removal or replacement candidate if:

- it adds little unique beginner value
- it mostly repeats nearby body content
- it competes with the CTA route
- it acts like a hidden sale / benefits / reassurance block
- it cannot stay within the `1095` role without repeated caveats

## 17. HOLD Continuation Conditions

Keep `1095` at `HOLD` if any of the following remains unresolved:

2. FAQ effectively turns `1095` into a `1106`-like benefits page
